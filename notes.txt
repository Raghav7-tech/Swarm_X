Blind Search (Dijkstra's): You check every street in a perfect circle around you, expanding outward until you hit the shop. It guarantees you find the closest path, but you waste time checking streets in the completely opposite direction.
Greedy Search: You only walk down streets that look like they point toward the shop. You might run into a dead-end or a wall and have to backtrack.
A Search:* You look at a street and say: "Okay, this street is short (low $g$) AND it points toward the shop (low $h$)." You pick that one. If you hit a wall, you remember the other promising streets you skipped and try the next best one.
